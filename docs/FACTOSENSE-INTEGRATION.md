# FactoSense Integration

## Current Scope

FactoSense is presented on the company website as a concise flagship-product highlight, not as a duplicated product website.

The dedicated product website remains the source for the complete product story:

- `https://www.factosense.com/`

## Company Website Placement

- `/index.html` includes one compact FactoSense product highlight after the company services/capabilities section and before the portfolio section.
- The homepage header includes a `Products` menu item that anchors to `#factosense-product`.
- The original homepage hero remains the first major section.
- No local FactoSense product page is used.

## CTA Routing

The single FactoSense CTA on the company website opens the official product website in a new tab:

- `https://www.factosense.com/`

## Content Boundary

The company website only introduces FactoSense as a Funoon Lab product. Product details, complete capabilities, use cases, technical information, product visuals and conversion journey should remain on the official FactoSense website.
